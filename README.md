01 — About Endorse
What is Endorse?
Endorse is an electronic signature and document management platform. It helps individuals and businesses create, send, sign, and manage documents digitally, replacing slow paper-based processes with a faster, simpler digital workflow.
What problem are we Endorse solve?
Traditional document workflows are slow and inconvenient: printing, signing by hand, scanning, emailing, waiting for a response, following up, and manually organizing files. This wastes time and creates friction for anyone who regularly deals with agreements, contracts, or forms. Endorse removes that friction by letting people send, sign, and manage documents entirely online.
Who is Endorse for?
Endorse is built for individuals and businesses who regularly create, send, sign, or manage documents, including:
•	Individuals and freelancers
•	Small businesses
•	Consultants and professionals
•	Agencies
•	Vendors and service providers
•	Startups and growing businesses
Why does Endorse exist?
Endorse exists because everyday document work — sending a contract, collecting a signature, keeping agreements organized — is still harder than it needs to be for smaller businesses and independent professionals. Endorse gives them a simple, affordable way to handle this without the complexity of enterprise-oriented tools.

02 — Product
Product overview
Endorse is a digital platform for handling documents from start to finish: creating or uploading a document, sending it to the right people, collecting signatures, and keeping everything organized in one place. It is designed to feel simple and approachable rather than heavy or enterprise-oriented.
What users can do with Endorse
•	Upload or create documents that need to be signed
•	Send documents to one or more recipients for signature
•	Sign documents electronically
•	Track the status of documents (sent, viewed, signed, completed)
•	Store and organize signed and pending documents in one place
•	Manage agreements without printing, scanning, or emailing back and forth
Key features
•	Digital document creation and sending
•	Electronic signing
•	Document status tracking
•	Centralized document storage and organization
Key benefits
Benefit	What it means for the user
Save time	No more printing, scanning, or manual follow-up on paperwork.
Reduce paperwork	Fewer physical documents to manage, file, and store.
Work remotely	Send and sign documents from anywhere, without needing to meet in person.
Simplify document workflows	One place to send, sign, and track documents instead of juggling email and paper.
Keep documents organized	Documents are stored and easy to find, instead of scattered across inboxes and folders.
Get documents signed more efficiently	Recipients can sign quickly online instead of printing and returning by hand.
Reduce unnecessary administrative work	Less manual chasing, filing, and re-sending of documents.
How Endorse works
At a high level, Endorse works in three steps:
•	1. Create or upload — the user prepares the document that needs to be signed.
•	2. Send — the document is sent to the relevant recipient(s) for review and signature.
•	3. Sign & manage — recipients sign electronically, and the document is tracked and stored in Endorse.
What makes Endorse different
Endorse is built to be simple and accessible rather than complex and enterprise-first. Where many e-signature tools are designed primarily around large organizations, Endorse focuses on individuals, freelancers, and small businesses who want something straightforward and affordable, without a steep learning curve.
Our value proposition
Endorse makes it simple to send, sign, and manage documents digitally, helping individuals and small businesses save time, reduce paperwork, and stay organized, without the cost or complexity of enterprise-focused tools.
Who should use Endorse
Anyone who regularly needs to send, sign, or manage documents but doesn't want a complicated or expensive system: freelancers sending contracts, small business owners managing agreements, consultants sending proposals, agencies handling client paperwork, vendors and service providers formalizing orders, and startups managing early operational documents.
When someone should use Endorse
•	When they need a document signed and don't want to print, scan, or mail it
•	When they're tired of manually tracking who has and hasn't signed a document
•	When they want their agreements stored in one organized place
•	When they're working remotely or with clients/partners who aren't in the same location
•	When they want a simple, affordable alternative to heavier enterprise tools
Product messaging
Product messaging should always connect a feature to the real problem it solves, rather than describing the feature on its own. For example, instead of "Endorse lets you track document status," say "Know exactly who still needs to sign, without chasing anyone down."
Marketing principles for talking about the product
•	Lead with the problem or benefit, not the feature name
•	Only describe features that are confirmed to exist in the current product
•	Avoid technical or legal claims (e.g. compliance, security certifications) unless confirmed
•	Keep language plain — write the way you'd explain it to a small business owner, not an IT department
•	Never imply Endorse has capabilities it does not yet have

05 — Brand Voice & Communication Guidelines
Brand personality
Simple, professional, friendly, modern, helpful, trustworthy, confident, and human.
Tone of voice
Endorse sounds like a knowledgeable, friendly professional — someone who understands the frustration of paperwork and explains things clearly, without being overly casual or overly corporate. Confident, but never boastful.
Writing style
•	Short, clear sentences
•	Everyday language over technical or legal jargon
•	Active voice
•	Direct and benefit-focused
•	Warm, not stiff — Endorse talks like a helpful person, not a corporation
How we speak to customers
We speak to customers the way a helpful, competent colleague would: clearly, respectfully, and without unnecessary formality. We explain, we don't lecture. We reassure, we don't oversell.
Words we like
•	Simple, easy, fast, organized, clear
•	Send, sign, manage, track
•	Save time, stay organized, get it done
•	Straightforward, reliable, practical
Words to avoid
•	Revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive, best-in-class
•	Synergy, leverage, seamless (overused), cutting-edge
•	Enterprise-grade (unless factually accurate and relevant)
•	Any absolute claims ("the best," "the only," "guaranteed") that cannot be substantiated
Grammar and formatting guidelines
•	Use sentence case for headlines, not Title Case
•	Use the Oxford comma for clarity
•	Keep sentences under ~20 words where possible
•	Use numerals for numbers (e.g. "3 steps," not "three steps") in marketing copy
•	Avoid exclamation points except when genuinely warranted
Social media voice
Friendly, conversational, and helpful. Slightly more casual than the website, but never sloppy. Educational and relatable — speaking to real frustrations with paperwork.
Advertising voice
Clear and benefit-led. Ads should state the problem and the benefit plainly, without exaggeration or hype. One idea per ad.
Email voice
Direct, respectful of the reader's time, and helpful. Emails should get to the point quickly and always have a clear next step.
Customer communication guidelines
•	Always be honest about what Endorse can and cannot currently do
•	Acknowledge frustration or confusion before offering a solution
•	Keep responses clear and jargon-free
•	Never overpromise timelines, features, or outcomes
Do's and Don'ts
Do	Don't
Speak plainly and clearly	Use jargon or overly technical language
Focus on real problems and benefits	Make exaggerated or unverifiable claims
Sound human and approachable	Sound like generic, robotic marketing copy
Be confident and direct	Be pushy or aggressive
Keep messaging consistent with this playbook	Improvise brand voice inconsistently across channels
Examples of weak vs. improved Endorse messaging
Weak	Improved
"Endorse is a revolutionary, next-generation e-signature solution."	"Endorse makes it simple to send, sign, and manage your documents."
"Leverage our seamless platform to optimize your document workflows."	"Stop chasing paperwork. Get documents signed in one simple place."
"The best e-signature tool on the market."	"A simple, affordable way to handle your documents — built for small businesses and freelancers."

A campaign is a focused set of marketing activities, with a defined goal, audience, timeline, and message, aimed at achieving a specific outcome (e.g. driving sign-ups, launching a feature, building awareness).
Types of campaigns Endorse may run
•	Awareness campaigns — introducing Endorse to a new audience
•	Acquisition campaigns — driving sign-ups
•	Product/feature launch campaigns — announcing something new [only when applicable]
•	Activation campaigns — encouraging new users to send/sign their first document
•	Retention campaigns — re-engaging existing users
•	Seasonal or timely campaigns — tied to relevant moments (e.g. new year, tax season) [TO BE CONFIRMED for relevance]
Campaign planning process
Step	What happens
Research	Understand the audience, problem, timing, and any relevant context before planning.
Planning	Define objective, audience, message, channels, timeline, and budget.
Content/creative production	Create and approve all assets needed for the campaign.
Launch	Publish and activate the campaign across chosen channels.
Monitoring	Track performance throughout the campaign and adjust as needed.
Analysis	Review results against the original objective and metrics.

11 — Marketing Workflow
Weekly
•	Review performance from the previous week (content and, if running, campaigns)
•	Create and schedule content for the upcoming week, per the content calendar
•	Publish scheduled content across active channels
•	Monitor engagement, comments, and messages, and respond where relevant
•	Note any quick wins, issues, or ideas for the following week
Monthly
•	Analyze the past month's performance against goals (Section 10)
•	Review content pillar mix and adjust based on what performed well
•	Plan the following month's content calendar and any campaigns
•	Share a brief summary/report with the founder or team
Campaign-based
Phase	Actions
Before	Write campaign brief, plan assets, confirm timeline and budget, prepare tracking.
During	Publish per plan, monitor performance daily/regularly, adjust if something isn't working.
After	Report results, document lessons learned, apply findings to future campaigns.
 
12 — Marketing Principles
•	Know the customer before creating the campaign.
•	Focus on problems, not just features.
•	Provide value before asking for something.
•	Keep messaging simple.
•	Do not exaggerate.
•	Test and learn.
•	Measure results.
•	Do not post just for the sake of posting.
•	Keep the brand human.
•	Use customer feedback to improve marketing.
•	Only communicate what is confirmed to be true about the product.
•	Prioritize a few things done well over many things done half-heartedly.
•	Consistency matters more than intensity — steady, sustainable effort beats short bursts.
